01 · About the project

Food, land and sovereignty

This project examines food sovereignty in Ecuador through an environmental, historical, social, and cultural lens.

It explores how colonial land systems, resource extraction, environmental change, Indigenous food systems, and grassroots organizing continue to shape Ecuador's relationship with food and land.

05 · Project outcome

From research to proposal

Our research resulted in a proposal for incorporating food sovereignty into the Pitzer in Ecuador Semester Program.

The proposal includes field-based learning opportunities and potential collaboration with local organizations working around food systems, Indigenous rights, agroecology, and environmental justice.

Translating research into an educational proposal.
