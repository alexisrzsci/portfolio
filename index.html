Hi, I’m Alexis

I’m an undergraduate researcher working at the intersection of geospatial science, environmental research and data — turning spatial data into maps, analyses and stories that make complex environmental questions easier to understand.

My academic interests sit between geography, environmental science, spatial analysis and visual communication. I have worked on GIS suitability models and spatial SQL in the Region of Murcia, an ArcGIS StoryMap on food sovereignty in Ecuador, and ecological research ranging from marine behaviour experiments to a ten-year study of beetle assemblages in Southern California.

What connects them is one question: how can spatial data help us see — and explain — the way people and ecosystems change?
