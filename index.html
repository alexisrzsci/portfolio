About me

I am an undergraduate researcher interested in geospatial science, GIS, environmental research, and the role of spatial data in understanding complex environmental systems.

My academic interests sit at the intersection of geography, environmental science, spatial analysis, and visual communication. I am particularly interested in transforming environmental data into clear maps, quantitative analyses, and research narratives that make complex questions easier to understand.
